# Paper 1 Venue and Replication Decision

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Decision status:** AAMAS-first route adopted; manuscript and zero-call experiment planning may proceed. No new target API calls have been made under this decision.

## Venue order

1. **AAMAS 2027 Main Track, COINE area — submit first and opt in to Findings.** This is the best time-sensitive route and the most direct area match for coordination, institutions, norms, natural-language communication, and accountable multi-agent systems. The official area guidance says to choose COINE when the social, institutional, normative, organizational, governance, or ethical dimension is central; GAAI is the secondary topical fit, but the paper does not contribute a new generative-agent architecture. The official deadlines are author registration `2026-09-17`, abstract `2026-10-01`, and paper `2026-10-08`, all Anywhere on Earth. The manuscript must fit eight main-text pages, with references unlimited.
2. **TMLR — conditional first fallback.** Prefer TMLR if AAMAS reviews accept technical soundness but reject on conference-level significance, breadth, or single-model generality. Its claim-support and audience-interest criteria, rolling review, and flexible length fit that failure mode.
3. **ACL Rolling Review — conditional NLP fallback.** Prefer the first eligible ARR cycle after the AAMAS decision if reviews instead indicate that the contribution belongs with LLM agents, computational social science, pragmatics, or evaluation. Do not dual-submit while AAMAS review is active.
4. **COLM 2027 — high-fit, slower option.** Use if a later version adds a genuine cross-family replication or a stronger language-mechanism disassembly and the timing is still worthwhile. Do not wait for COLM merely because its topic wording is closest.
5. **ICML/ICLR — defer.** A single hosted serving identity and no new learning method leave a predictable external-validity objection. A genuine cross-family replication would materially change that assessment.

## AAMAS Findings and submission consequences

Findings is not a separate track or a longer-paper option. Papers not selected for the AAMAS Proceedings are automatically considered for Findings unless the authors opt out at submission. Proceedings and Findings papers use the same eight-page format; both are peer-reviewed, archival, and citable. Therefore:

- opt in, because the project values a timely archival decision;
- write one self-contained eight-page paper rather than a larger “Findings version”;
- treat a Findings acceptance as publication of this contribution, not as permission to resubmit substantially the same full paper elsewhere;
- use supplementary material for auditability, while keeping all evidence essential to the claims in the main eight pages.

Official sources: [AAMAS submission instructions](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/instructions/) and [Findings of AAMAS 2027](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/findings/).

The current provider-bracketed V4.1 Flash era has an unknown remaining lifetime. The provider has already demonstrated that a stable requested model name does not guarantee a stable serving identity. Any experiment intended to pool with W-RG/W-SGB must therefore be completed while pre/post fingerprints still match the recorded era baseline. ## Historical cross-family evidence already available

The exact gift and money public-message constants, prompt builder, rules, environment parameters, and decoding settings are reused across the grok-4.5 and V4.1 Flash eras. In the three historical grok-4.5 seeds shared by the gift and money studies, gift-minus-money welfare is `+1.7500`, `+1.6250`, and `+0.1875` (mean `+1.1875`; 3/3 positive). W-CO later estimates `+2.3333` in the same direction under V4.1 Flash.

This is **exploratory cross-family directional concordance**, not a formal cross-model replication. The grok-era comparison was not prospectively paired or provider-bracketed; its realized meeting trajectories and dynamic prompts differ across arms. It supports a narrower limitations statement: the welfare ordering has appeared in two model families, whereas the H–H execution mechanism and semantic boundary remain single-era causal findings.

## Why `deepseek-v4-pro` is not the second model

DeepSeek announced that from 04:00 UTC on 2026-09-14, `deepseek-v4-pro` requests route to V4.1 Flash until V4.1 Pro launches. Therefore:

- `deepseek-v4-pro` and `deepseek-flash` are not independent model arms after the cutoff;
- a different requested string does not count as a different model;
- the pre-cutoff Pro control remains valid for diagnosing the earlier serving collapse but cannot be extended into a post-cutoff replication;
- the project must not describe current Pro calls as cross-model evidence.

Official source: [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash announcement](https://deepseek.com/en/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash/).

## Admission gate for a genuine second-model replication

Before freezing or spending target calls, a candidate must pass all of the following:

1. its catalog entry is distinct from `deepseek-flash`;
2. the response returns a distinct model identifier and stable fingerprint or equivalent serving metadata;
3. an exact historical positive action control is non-degenerate;
4. an unrelated negative/dominance control behaves correctly;
5. repeated distinct prompts show adequate parsed-output diversity;
6. pre/post brackets use `baseline-establish` on the first study and `baseline-compare` thereafter;
7. all target data remain separate from both historical `deepseek-v4-flash` and current V4.1 Flash estimates.

Passing these checks establishes an observed serving identity, not immutable weights or architectural independence.

## Replication sequence once a candidate exists

1. Run a small, disclosed bridge set of previously used prompts to measure behavioral discontinuity; do not pool it with either era.
2. Reproduce the engaged W-SGB core first: neutral, exact gift, Easy-only, and harmful E–E. This directly tests the paper's scope-expansion claim. Preserve fresh seeds, paired schedules, negative engagement, H–H swap rate, welfare, and familywise control.
3. Add the full seven-arm W-SGB set if a direct paper-level replication is required.
4. Reproduce W-RG if the submission needs cross-family support for the welfare-carrying execution channel as well as the scope boundary.
5. Treat priced exchange as a separate repair study. Its money package must pass a prospective named-sale engagement gate before any null is interpreted semantically.

The core four-arm scope replication and the engagement-gated money repair answer different questions and should not be merged after seeing outcomes.
