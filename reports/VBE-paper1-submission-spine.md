## One-sentence claim

In a language-model economy, a public rule can expand beyond its named roles and change welfare, but scope propagation, physical execution, and institutional quality are separate causal layers.

## Submission abstract draft

When language-model agents follow a public rule, literal compliance need not reveal the operative transaction or welfare mechanism. We study this problem in the Verification Budget Economy, an eight-agent repeated-exchange environment with scarce checks and intrinsically worthless transferable marks. A byte-identical-prompt execution intervention shows that a directive naming Easy-to-Hard gifts improves welfare mainly through unmentioned Hard-to-Hard check circulation: disabling that channel lowers mean welfare by 3.2083 points per account. A prospectively frozen seven-arm study then replicates the gift directive against no recommendation on Hard-to-Hard swap rate (+0.6070) and welfare (+2.0625). An engaged, mechanically harmful Easy-to-Easy directive also expands into unmentioned roles, while explicit “Easy only” actor scope blocks expansion. The exact money package produces no named sales and therefore cannot identify a priced-semantics boundary. The supported finding is package-level scope expansion among engaged uncompensated-transfer directives, constrained by subject exclusivity. Propagation strength, physical execution, private belief, and institutional welfare remain distinct.

## The three defensible contributions

1. **A causal rule-to-execution decomposition.** W-RG holds the visible gift prompt byte-identical and intervenes only after model response. Blocking H–H check giving removes 3.2083 welfare points; the named E→H path was largely absorbed upstream by Hard reciprocity. This identifies a dynamic execution-channel total effect rather than a natural-mediation proportion.

2. **A prospective scope boundary.** W-SGB uses fresh seeds, balanced seven-arm order, two co-primary endpoints, Holm-controlled contrast families, a manipulation-valid negative directive, and a healthy provider bracket. The result is generic spillover among engaged uncompensated-transfer directives with subject-exclusivity binding; a helping-specific increment is possible but not established at the declared MRES.

3. **A systems-layer implication.** The broader E/I/P evidence shows why rule visibility cannot stand in for belief, authorship, persistence, or execution. In particular, the public ledger is a readable policy carrier and the execution transform is a separate causal layer. W-SGB adds that even successful scope propagation is not a welfare guarantee.

## Main-text architecture

Target the AAMAS eight-page main-text limit; references may extend beyond it, but an in-paper appendix may not.

1. **Introduction — 0.9 page.** Begin with the literal-rule fallacy, state scope propagation → execution → welfare, and list the three contributions.
2. **Environment, formal object, and estimands — 1.1 pages.** Define the named transaction support `(actor, partner, transfer, scope constraints)`, opportunity-normalized executed-relation rates, and the seed-level estimand under interference.
3. **Causal identification — 1.3 pages.** Present W-RG's identical-prompt post-response intervention and W-SGB's balanced message intervention, negative engagement gate, multiplicity control, and compact provider bracket.
4. **Main results — 2.6 pages.** Lead with Figure 1. Keep the compact W-RG table and W-SGB co-primary table. W-CO receives only the discovery facts needed to motivate H–H intervention.
5. **Discussion and AAMAS connection — 1.0 page.** Explain why actor exclusivity binds while beneficiary exclusivity permits H–H execution; separate rule propagation from institutional quality and connect to role-indexed normative systems.
6. **Exploratory frozen-trace diagnostics — 1.0 page.** Full role–relation matrix, four-round H–H dynamics, and account-score distribution, explicitly outside the prospective contrast families.
7. **Limitations and threat model — 0.8 page.** Single provider-labeled causal era, only three weak historical cross-family seeds, package-level wording, simplified economy, project-internal freezing, and no private-state claim.
8. **Conclusion and reproducibility — 0.3 page.** State the bounded result and point to frozen artifacts. Prior-era E/I/P tables and routing chronology remain supplementary.
